# Quality of Death

## Gravestone Mod

When you die, the [Gravestone Mod](https://modrinth.com/mod/gravestone-mod) places a grave at your position.

The recommended way to get your items back is to crouch on the grave.

You can also just break the grave, but if you're near lava this can be riskier!

Every time you die you get an obituary, which contains information about when and where you died and what you had in your inventory.

If you can't get your grave back, talk to kayila or duckinator and we'll sort it out for you!
