# Getting Started

## Basic Starting Resources

* Archwood (5 types getting some saplings and logs of each is ideal)
* Gold
* Glass
* Lapis and/or Amethyst (get ready to store as much as you can, you’ll need a lot as they make **Source Gems** which are a staple crafting item)
* Diamonds (only a couple to start \- need increases you get to mid-late tier crafting)

## Starting Structures/Items

* **Imbuement Chamber** \- this transmutes some items to source gems or essences for crafting.
* **Inscription Table** \- crafts glyphs for casting from mundane items and experience
* **SourceLink** (agronomic, mycelial and/or vitalic) \- creates source (your magical “fuel”) from different means
* **Source Jars** (5ish is a good start) \- where you store your source
* **Arcane Pedestals or Platforms** (3 for Imbuement, 8 for Enchanting) \- used for placing items in magical crafting rituals
* **Novice Spellbook** (iron sword, shovel, axe, and pickaxe with a book on a crafting table)
* **Enchanting Apparatus and Arcane Core** \- 2 block structure needed for some of the mid-tier crafting but really, good to get it setup anyways so you know how much space you need.
