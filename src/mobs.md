# Mobs &amp; How To Harass Them

## Giants

Some zombies are giant, courtesy of [Giant Spawn](https://modrinth.com/mod/giant-spawn).

## Goblin Traders

We have [Goblin Traders](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/goblin-traders)!

## Mowzie's Mobs

[Mowzie's Mobs](https://modrinth.com/mod/mowzies-mobs) adds a ton of various mobs.

## How To Harass Mobs

- [Snowballs Freeze Mobs](https://modrinth.com/mod/snowballs-freeze-mobs), for a bit
- [No Hostiles Around Campfire](https://modrinth.com/mod/no-hostiles-around-campfire) prevents hostile mob spawns, and burns existing hostile mobs, in an area around the campfire
