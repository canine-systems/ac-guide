# EMI

This modpack includes [EMI](https://modrinth.com/mod/emi) for viewing items and recipes.

If you've used JEI, REI, NEI, or similar, it will feel familiar.

In addition to EMI itself, we have the following mods which add information to EMI:

- [EMI Enchanting](https://modrinth.com/mod/emi-enchanting): enchantment information
- [EMI Loot](https://modrinth.com/mod/emi-loot): loot drop information
- [EMI Ores](https://modrinth.com/mod/emi-ores): ore generation information
- [EMI Professions](https://modrinth.com/mod/emi-professions-(emip)): information on which workstations are required by which Villager Profession
- [EMIffect](https://modrinth.com/mod/emiffect): information on status effects

We also have [EMI: Create Schematics](https://modrinth.com/mod/emi-create-schematics), which lets you take a Clipboard from Create and add schematic items to your EMI Favourites.

## Hand Over Your Items

You can now [Hand Over Your Items](https://modrinth.com/mod/hand-over-your-items) without dropping
them on the ground.

By crouching and right-clicking another player, the entire item stack in your main hand is given to them.
If there is no room in their inventory, the items will be dropped on the ground.

Both players will receive a message indicating they have given or received items.

## Inventory Mending

If you have an item that's enchanted with mending, they will repair themselves _anywhere in your inventory_ (not just when equiped) via [Inventory Mending](https://modrinth.com/mod/inventory-mending).
