# Waystones

We have [Waystones](https://modrinth.com/mod/waystones) to allow you to teleport to
places you've encountered previously.

Waypoints can be configured to be accessible to everyone. An example of this is the Waypoint
at Aura (the spawn area).

In addition to being craftable, they are also added to most(?) Villages.

Waystones can be visible on your map, thanks to [Xaero's Maps x Waystones](https://modrinth.com/mod/xaeros-maps-x-waystones].
