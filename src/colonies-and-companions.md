# Colonies &amp; Companions

Our server's got a decent number of players. When it comes to making friends of the non-player
variety, our modpack is built around the following:

- [MineColonies](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/minecolonies): start your own colony!
- [Modern Companions](https://modrinth.com/mod/modern-companions): ~~kidnap someone and force them to protect you~~ find a new friend and go on grand adventures!
- [Towns and towers](https://modrinth.com/mod/towns-and-towers): more village varieties.
- [Dungeons and Taverns](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/dungeon-and-taverns): adds things like fire watch towers, taverns, and dungeons.

We also have the following add-on mods for MineColonies:
- [Better With Minecolonies](https://modrinth.com/mod/better-with-minecolonies): adds compatibility recipes for MineColonies
- [Byzantine Styles Pack for Minecolonies](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/byzantine-styles-pack-for-minecolonies): adds building styles for MineColonies.
- [Compatibility addon for MineColonies](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/minecolonies-compatibility): adds and tweaks jobs, removes saturation nerf on MineColonies cooked foods, few other things.
- [SmallColonies for Minecolonies](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/smallcolonies): adds additional colony blueprints.
- [Stylecolonies](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/stylecolonies): adds additional colony blueprints.
- [TownTalk](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/towntalk): more voice lines for MineColonies.
- [Tweaks addon for MineColonies](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/minecolonies-tweaks): adds config options, commands, items, tools, also removes sautation nerf on cooked food (lol), bunch of other stuff.
